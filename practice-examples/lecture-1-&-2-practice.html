Lecture 1 & 2 - Practice

Q1: Properties

اعمل Facts للجمل الانجليزي دي:

  1. Gold is valuable.
  2. Saly is a female.
  3. Aly owns some gold.
  4. Gamal gives the book to Mona. (3 arguments)
Solution (Properties)
valuable(gold).
female(saly).
owns(aly, gold).
gives(gamal, book, mona).

Q2: Facts Translation

اعمل Facts لجمل الانجليزي دي:

  1. A car is fun.
  2. A rose is red.
  3. Bill likes Cindy.
  4. Cindy likes Bill.
  5. Bill likes dogs.
Solution (Facts Translation)
fun(car).
red(rose).
likes(bill, cindy).
likes(cindy, bill).
likes(bill, dogs).

Q3: Age Database

اعمل قاعدة بيانات للداتا دي:

  1. Aly is 42 years old.
  2. Hany is 15 years old.
  3. Saly is 20 years old.
  4. Aly is male.
  5. Hany is male.
  6. Saly is f1.emale.

واكتب الاستعلامات دي:

  1. Is Aly 42 years old?
  2. What is Hany's age?
  3. Who is male? (اعرض كل الإجابات)
  4. List names with age < 40.
  5. List names with age < 40 and they are female.
Solution (Age Database)
age(aly, 42).
age(hany, 15).
age(saly, 20).
male(aly).
male(hany).
female(saly).

Queries:

?- age(aly, 42).    % true
?- age(hany, X).    % X = 15
?- male(Y).         % Y = hany ; Y = aly

?- age(Name, Age), Age < 40.
% Name = hany, Age = 15 ;
% Name = saly, Age = 20

?- age(Name, Age), Age < 40, female(Name).
% Name = saly, Age = 20

Q5: Conjunctions

من قاعدة البيانات:

likes(mary, food).
likes(mary, tea).
likes(john, tea).
likes(john, mary).

اكتب الاستعلامات دي باستخدام الـ conjunctions ا ( and , or ):

  1. Does John like Mary and does Mary like John?
  2. Is there anything that John and Mary both like?
Solution (Conjunctions)
?- likes(john, mary), likes(mary, john).
% no :(

?- likes(mary, X), likes(john, X).
% X = tea

Q6: Jim's Family

اعمل قاعدة بيانات لعيلة Jim:

  1. John is the father of Jim.
  2. Jane is the mother of Jim.
  3. Jack is father of John.

واكتب الاستعلامات:

  1. Who is Jim's father?
  2. Is Jane the mother of Fred?
  3. Is Jane the mother of Jim?
  4. Does Jack have a grandchild?
Solution (Jim's Family)
father(john, jim).
mother(jane, jim).
father(jack, john).

Queries:

?- father(X, jim).
?- mother(jane, fred).
?- mother(jane, jim).
?- father(jack, X), father(X, Y).
